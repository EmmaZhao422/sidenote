Use Sidenote with Claude

If you already use Claude, you don't need anything else to get real answers to the thoughts you park. The sidenote-coach skill teaches Claude what to do with them. It runs in your own Claude account, on your own plan, so it costs this site nothing and needs no API key.

What it does

  • Goes through your parked thoughts. Paste your parked thoughts, or attach a backup file. Questions get answered, to-dos get a first step, worries get a calmer second look, and you get a short "next ten minutes" list.
  • Reviews your week. From a backup file it reports your focus time, focus score, what your stray thoughts were about, the minute of a block they tend to hit, and which scene you focus best with. The numbers are computed by a script, the same way this site computes them.
  • Plans a block in under a minute: one checkable goal, two or three steps, a starting line.
  • Holds your thoughts in the chat when you study without this site open. It replies with a tick and nothing else until you say you're done.

Get the skill

Download sidenote-coach.zip

A small ZIP file. It contains plain-text instructions and one short Python script; you can read all of it on GitHub before adding it.

Add it to Claude

  1. In Claude, open Customize, then Skills.
  2. Click the + button, choose Create skill, then Upload a skill.
  3. Pick the ZIP file you downloaded. Leave it zipped.
  4. Make sure the skill is switched on in the list.

Skills need "Code execution and file creation" switched on in Claude's settings. Claude's menus change from time to time; if these steps don't match what you see, Claude's help page on skills has the current ones.

Use it

  • After a study block: on the Thoughts page here, click Copy for Claude, then paste into a Claude chat.
  • For a weekly review: in Settings here, click Save a backup file, attach that file in Claude and ask "How did my week go?"
  • Before studying: tell Claude "Help me plan a 45-minute block for chapter 7."
  • Without this site: tell Claude "I'm starting a 25-minute block, hold my thoughts."

What to know

  • Whatever you paste or attach goes to Claude under your own account and its privacy terms. This site never sees it.
  • A chat can't keep time or play sound. The timer, the scenes and the charts stay here.
  • Sidenote is an independent student project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic, the company that makes Claude.
