Privacy

Last updated November 21, 2026

The short version: there is no account, your notes stay in your browser, and the text of a thought leaves your device only when you ask the AI to deal with it.

What is stored, and where

Everything you enter (parked thoughts, study goals and steps, session history, ratings and settings) is saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. We don't have a database of users and we can't see this data. Clearing your browser data erases it. You can download a backup or erase everything from the Settings page.

What is sent to an AI model

When you click to have a thought dealt with, ask for suggested steps, or ask for an AI-written recap, the relevant text is sent to an AI model so it can reply:

  • Dealing with a thought: the text of that thought and the goal of the study block it came from.
  • Suggested steps: the goal you typed and the length of the block.
  • AI recap: your summary numbers and the goals you typed. The thoughts themselves are not included.

Depending on how the site is set up, the request goes either through this site's own server, which forwards it to the model provider and does not store the text (it keeps only a count of requests, to stop abuse; Cloudflare, which runs that server, may log technical details such as IP addresses), or, if you entered your own API key in Settings, straight from your browser to the provider you chose. As of the date above, this site's own server connection is switched off, so a thought reaches an AI only through your own key, the Claude app, or the "Ask" buttons described below. The provider handles that text under its own terms and privacy policy. Nothing is sent until you click, so please don't park anything you wouldn't want processed that way and then ask the AI about it.

If you paste in your own API key, it is kept only in the tab you pasted it into, until you close that tab, unless you tick "Remember the key on this device". It is sent only to the provider you chose, and it is never written into a backup file.

Advertising

Sidenote is free, and some pages may show ads to pay for running it. As of the date above, no ads are running. Ads are never shown while you are planning or in a study block. When ads are enabled they will be provided by Google AdSense, and then:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info.

Google explains what it collects on sites that show its ads in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Where the law requires consent (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), ads will not be switched on for visitors there until a consent banner is in place that asks before advertising cookies are used. The site's own code sets no cookies. The content of your notes is never shared with advertisers.

Other services involved

  • Hosting: the site is served by GitHub Pages, which logs technical information such as IP addresses.
  • Fonts: the typefaces are files on this site. No request goes to a font service.
  • Offline copy: your browser keeps a copy of the site's own files so it can open without a connection. That copy holds no notes.
  • "Search the web", "Ask Claude" and "Ask ChatGPT": these buttons open the other site in a new tab with the text of that one thought (up to 500 characters) in the link. "Ask Claude about all" does the same with every thought that is still waiting. Nothing is sent unless you press the button, and what happens next is covered by that site's own privacy policy.

Sidenote doesn't run tracking scripts. The site is able to count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and does not see what you write. As of the date above, that counter is switched off. If you open Sidenote inside the Claude app, a thought you ask to have dealt with is sent to Claude there, under your own Claude account.

Children

Sidenote is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.
