Mind wandered? Park it here.

Park every stray thought in one line while you study. When the block ends, go through them one by one, then see where your attention went.

How a study block goes

  1. Five minutes to get ready. Write what this block is for, split it into two or three steps, and empty out whatever is already on your mind.
  2. Study with one box open. A timer, a background scene, and one input box. A thought shows up, you type it, press Enter, and go back to work.
  3. Deal with the thoughts afterwards. Questions get an answer, to-dos get a day or a first step, worries get a calmer second look.
  4. See the pattern. The summary shows what you worked on, how steady each block was, and what your stray thoughts are usually about.

Sidenote is free and needs no account. Your notes are saved in this browser and nowhere else.
